## About

I'm an informatics student who likes the moment a rough idea turns into something people can actually click on.
I move between frontend and backend depending on what the project needs, and I care a lot about the small details: spacing, motion, the way a button feels when you press it.

Right now I'm digging into cleaner architecture and scalable patterns, mostly by building things and breaking them.
